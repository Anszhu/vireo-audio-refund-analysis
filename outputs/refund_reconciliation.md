## Why Finance sees "well over a crore a quarter"
* **Legacy Freshdesk stores money in paise (×100).** Evidence, not assumption: on all 125 re-imported tickets carrying a refund, the legacy value is exactly 100× the helpdesk value (100%); the median refund-to-retail-price ratio is 0.90 for helpdesk rows but 90.0 for legacy rows; and amounts typed into agent notes match the refund field on 1,501 of 1,503 refund notes that quote a rupee figure once legacy is divided by 100.
* The three quarters before the 14 Sep 2025 go-live are legacy-dominated, so they show ₹6–9 crore each in the raw export. Quarters after go-live (helpdesk only) are already in rupees and show ₹16.3, 12.6 and 12.8 lakh.
* **Two factors, not one.** (1) Legacy paise: every `legacy_fd` row is 100x too large (775 refund rows incl. re-imports; legacy raw value ₹22.53 crore). (2) Re-import duplicates: 638 ticket_ids appear under both systems, 125 of the duplicate rows carry a refund. **How the gap is split between them depends on the order of the bridge.** Removing duplicates first (as in the table above) attributes ₹3.61 crore to duplicates (15.7% of the raw total) and ₹18.73 crore to paise scaling. Scaling all legacy rows first attributes ₹22.31 crore to scaling and only ₹3.61 lakh to the duplicates (they are then already in rupees). Under either order the paise unit is the dominant cause and the duplicates are real but secondary; the bridge total is the same.

## Does it reconcile to the helpdesk's "≈ ₹11 lakh a quarter"?
* Canonical total ₹67.10 lakh ÷ 6 quarters = **₹11.18 lakh per quarter** – consistent with "around ₹11 lakh".
* But the quarters are not flat: 2025Q1 ₹6.10 lakh, 2025Q2 ₹7.27 lakh, 2025Q3 ₹12.07 lakh, 2025Q4 ₹16.28 lakh, 2026Q1 ₹12.58 lakh, 2026Q2 ₹12.80 lakh. The last three quarters average ₹13.89 lakh.
* **Not established from the supplied data:** how the helpdesk's own report computes ₹11 lakh (period, basis, treatment of the re-import). We show that the cleaned data is consistent with it as a six-quarter average, not that this is how it was produced.

## Other reconciliation notes
* Refund rows: canonical helpdesk-native 1690 rows (₹48.18 lakh); legacy_fd 650 rows (₹18.92 lakh) after removing the re-imported copies.
* Refund + replacement double-counting is a *policy* issue (see exceptions file), not a *summation* issue: each ticket carries one refund amount, so replacements do not add to the refund total.
* Refund amounts on open/pending tickets are included in totals (they are raised refunds); refund *rates* use resolved/closed tickets as denominators.
